# Verbatim Medical Intake Demo — Cardiology

## Scenario

A Spanish-speaking patient visits a primary care clinic with exertional chest pressure and shortness of breath. Verbatim conducts the intake in Spanish, provides an English translation, summarizes the encounter, and communicates a cardiology referral after physician review.

---

## Conversation

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Hola. Soy **Verbatim**, su asistente de admisión médica. Voy a hacerle algunas preguntas para ayudar a preparar su consulta. ¿Cuál es el motivo principal de su visita hoy?

**English**

> Hello. I'm **Verbatim**, your medical intake assistant. I'll ask you a few questions to help prepare for your appointment. What brings you in today?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Desde hace dos semanas siento una presión fuerte en el pecho cuando camino o subo escaleras.

**English**

> For the past two weeks I've felt strong pressure in my chest when I walk or climb stairs.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Entiendo. ¿Esa presión desaparece cuando descansa?

**English**

> I understand. Does that pressure go away when you rest?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Sí. Cuando me siento, mejora en unos minutos.

**English**

> Yes. When I sit down, it improves within a few minutes.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> En una escala del cero al diez, donde diez es el peor dolor imaginable, ¿qué tan intensa es la presión cuando aparece?

**English**

> On a scale from zero to ten, where ten is the worst pain imaginable, how intense is the pressure when it occurs?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Es un siete.

**English**

> It's about a seven.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿El dolor o la presión se irradia hacia el brazo, el cuello o la mandíbula?

**English**

> Does the pain or pressure radiate to your arm, neck, or jaw?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> A veces se siente en el brazo izquierdo.

**English**

> Sometimes I feel it in my left arm.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Ha tenido falta de aire, sudoración o náuseas junto con la presión en el pecho?

**English**

> Have you had shortness of breath, sweating, or nausea along with the chest pressure?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Sí. Me falta el aire cuando camino rápido, y a veces sudo. No tengo náuseas.

**English**

> Yes. I get short of breath when I walk quickly, and sometimes I sweat. I don't have nausea.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Ha tenido estos síntomas en reposo o durante la noche?

**English**

> Have you had these symptoms at rest or during the night?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> No. Solo cuando hago esfuerzo.

**English**

> No. Only when I exert myself.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Está tomando algún medicamento actualmente?

**English**

> Are you currently taking any medications?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Sí. Tomo lisinopril para la presión alta y una aspirina diaria.

**English**

> Yes. I take lisinopril for high blood pressure and a daily aspirin.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Tiene alguna alergia a medicamentos?

**English**

> Do you have any medication allergies?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Soy alérgico a la sulfa.

**English**

> I'm allergic to sulfa.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Gracias. Permítame confirmar que entendí correctamente.
>
> Usted ha tenido presión fuerte en el pecho durante dos semanas, principalmente con el esfuerzo, con una intensidad de siete sobre diez. Mejora con el reposo. A veces se irradia al brazo izquierdo. También presenta falta de aire y sudoración con el esfuerzo. Usted toma lisinopril y aspirina diaria, y es alérgico a la sulfa.
>
> ¿Es correcto?

**English**

> Thank you. Let me confirm I understood correctly.
>
> You've had strong chest pressure for two weeks, mainly with exertion, rated seven out of ten. It improves with rest. It sometimes radiates to the left arm. You've also had shortness of breath and sweating with exertion. You take lisinopril and daily aspirin, and you are allergic to sulfa.
>
> Is that correct?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Sí, es correcto.

**English**

> Yes, that's correct.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Gracias. He preparado un resumen para su médico. Con base en los síntomas que ha informado, **su médico recomienda una evaluación con un cardiólogo** para estudiar la causa de su dolor de pecho. Nuestro personal le ayudará a programar la cita con el especialista.

**English**

> Thank you. I've prepared a summary for your physician. Based on the symptoms you've reported, **your physician recommends an evaluation with a cardiologist** to further investigate the cause of your chest pain. Our staff will help schedule your appointment with the specialist.

---

## Physician Summary

### Chief Complaint

- Exertional chest pressure

### Duration

- 2 weeks

### Severity

- 7/10

### Symptoms

- Chest pressure with walking or climbing stairs
- Improves with rest
- Occasional radiation to left arm
- Shortness of breath with exertion
- Sweating with exertion
- No symptoms at rest or at night
- No nausea

### Medical History

- Hypertension

### Medication

- Lisinopril
- Daily aspirin

### Allergy

- Sulfa

---

## Referral

**Specialist:** Cardiology

**Reason for Referral:**

- Exertional chest pressure for two weeks
- Symptoms improve with rest
- Radiation to left arm
- Associated shortness of breath and sweating

**Status:** Referral sent to Cardiology for further evaluation.

---

## Clinical Safety Note

Verbatim supports intake, translation, summarization, and clinical decision support. Final diagnosis, treatment, and referral decisions must be reviewed and approved by a licensed healthcare professional.
