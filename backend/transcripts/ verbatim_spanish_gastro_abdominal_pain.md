# Verbatim Medical Intake Demo - Gastrology

## Scenario

A Spanish-speaking patient visits a primary care clinic with persistent upper abdominal pain. Verbatim conducts the intake in Spanish, provides an English translation, summarizes the encounter, and communicates a gastroenterology referral after physician review.
