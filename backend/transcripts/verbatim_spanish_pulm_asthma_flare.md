# Verbatim Medical Intake Demo — Pulmonology

## Scenario

A Spanish-speaking patient visits a primary care clinic with a worsening asthma flare, nighttime wheezing, and frequent rescue inhaler use. Verbatim conducts the intake in Spanish, provides an English translation, summarizes the encounter, and communicates a pulmonology referral after physician review.

---

## Conversation

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Hola. Soy **Verbatim**, su asistente de admisión médica. Voy a hacerle algunas preguntas para ayudar a preparar su consulta. ¿Cuál es el motivo principal de su visita hoy?

**English**

> Hello. I'm **Verbatim**, your medical intake assistant. I'll ask you a few questions to help prepare for your appointment. What brings you in today?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Tengo asma y desde hace tres semanas me cuesta mucho respirar, sobre todo por la noche.

**English**

> I have asthma and for the past three weeks I've had a hard time breathing, especially at night.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Entiendo. ¿Ha notado silbidos en el pecho o tos junto con la falta de aire?

**English**

> I understand. Have you noticed wheezing in your chest or cough along with the shortness of breath?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Sí. Silbo mucho por la noche y toso hasta que me despierto.

**English**

> Yes. I wheeze a lot at night and cough until I wake up.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> En una escala del cero al diez, donde diez es la peor dificultad para respirar imaginable, ¿qué tan intensa es ahora?

**English**

> On a scale from zero to ten, where ten is the worst breathing difficulty imaginable, how severe is it now?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Durante el día es un cinco. Por la noche llega a un ocho.

**English**

> During the day it's a five. At night it gets up to an eight.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Con qué frecuencia usa su inhalador de rescate en estos días?

**English**

> How often are you using your rescue inhaler these days?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Lo uso cuatro o cinco veces al día. Antes solo lo usaba una o dos veces por semana.

**English**

> I use it four or five times a day. Before, I only used it once or twice a week.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Ha tenido fiebre, dolor en el pecho o síntomas que no mejoran después del inhalador?

**English**

> Have you had fever, chest pain, or symptoms that do not improve after the inhaler?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> No tengo fiebre. El inhalador me ayuda un poco, pero el alivio dura poco. No tengo dolor fuerte en el pecho.

**English**

> I don't have a fever. The inhaler helps a little, but the relief doesn't last long. I don't have strong chest pain.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Algo en su entorno ha cambiado recientemente, como polvo, humo, mascotas o un resfriado?

**English**

> Has anything in your environment changed recently, such as dust, smoke, pets, or a cold?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Empecé a trabajar cerca de obras con mucho polvo hace un mes.

**English**

> I started working near construction sites with a lot of dust a month ago.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Está tomando algún medicamento actualmente?

**English**

> Are you currently taking any medications?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Uso fluticasona inhalada todos los días y albuterol de rescate. También tomo loratadina para las alergias.

**English**

> I use inhaled fluticasone every day and albuterol for rescue. I also take loratadine for allergies.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Tiene alguna alergia a medicamentos?

**English**

> Do you have any medication allergies?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Soy alérgico a la aspirina; me provoca sibilancias.

**English**

> I'm allergic to aspirin; it causes wheezing.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Gracias. Permítame confirmar que entendí correctamente.
>
> Usted tiene asma y desde hace tres semanas presenta dificultad para respirar, peor por la noche, con silbidos y tos que lo despierta. La dificultad es de cinco sobre diez de día y ocho de noche. Usa el inhalador de rescate cuatro o cinco veces al día. El inhalador ayuda poco y por poco tiempo. Empezó a trabajar cerca de polvo de construcción hace un mes. Usa fluticasona e albuterol, toma loratadina, y es alérgico a la aspirina.
>
> ¿Es correcto?

**English**

> Thank you. Let me confirm I understood correctly.
>
> You have asthma and for three weeks have had breathing difficulty, worse at night, with wheezing and cough that wakes you. Difficulty is five out of ten by day and eight at night. You use your rescue inhaler four or five times a day. The inhaler helps only a little and briefly. You started working near construction dust a month ago. You use fluticasone and albuterol, take loratadine, and are allergic to aspirin.
>
> Is that correct?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Sí, es correcto.

**English**

> Yes, that's correct.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Gracias. He preparado un resumen para su médico. Con base en los síntomas que ha informado, **su médico recomienda una evaluación con un neumólogo** para estudiar el control de su asma. Nuestro personal le ayudará a programar la cita con el especialista.

**English**

> Thank you. I've prepared a summary for your physician. Based on the symptoms you've reported, **your physician recommends an evaluation with a pulmonologist** to further assess your asthma control. Our staff will help schedule your appointment with the specialist.

---

## Physician Summary

### Chief Complaint

- Worsening asthma / nighttime breathing difficulty

### Duration

- 3 weeks

### Severity

- 5/10 daytime; 8/10 nighttime

### Symptoms

- Nighttime wheezing and cough awakening patient
- Rescue inhaler use 4–5 times daily (baseline 1–2 times weekly)
- Partial, short-lived relief from rescue inhaler
- No fever; no severe chest pain
- Recent occupational dust exposure (construction)

### Medical History

- Asthma
- Environmental allergies

### Medication

- Inhaled fluticasone (daily)
- Albuterol (rescue)
- Loratadine

### Allergy

- Aspirin (causes wheezing)

---

## Referral

**Specialist:** Pulmonology

**Reason for Referral:**

- Asthma symptoms worsening for three weeks
- Frequent nighttime wheezing and cough
- Marked increase in rescue inhaler use
- Possible occupational dust trigger
- Incomplete symptom control on current regimen

**Status:** Referral sent to Pulmonology for further evaluation.

---

## Clinical Safety Note

Verbatim supports intake, translation, summarization, and clinical decision support. Final diagnosis, treatment, and referral decisions must be reviewed and approved by a licensed healthcare professional.
