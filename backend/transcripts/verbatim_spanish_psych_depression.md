# Verbatim Medical Intake Demo — Behavioral Health

## Scenario

A Spanish-speaking patient visits a primary care clinic with low mood, sleep and appetite changes, and difficulty functioning at work. Verbatim conducts the intake in Spanish, provides an English translation, summarizes the encounter, and communicates a behavioral health referral after physician review.

---

## Conversation

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Hola. Soy **Verbatim**, su asistente de admisión médica. Voy a hacerle algunas preguntas para ayudar a preparar su consulta. ¿Cuál es el motivo principal de su visita hoy?

**English**

> Hello. I'm **Verbatim**, your medical intake assistant. I'll ask you a few questions to help prepare for your appointment. What brings you in today?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Desde hace unos dos meses me siento muy triste casi todos los días y ya no tengo ganas de hacer nada.

**English**

> For about two months I've felt very sad almost every day and I don't feel like doing anything anymore.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Entiendo. ¿Ha notado cambios en el sueño o en el apetito?

**English**

> I understand. Have you noticed changes in your sleep or appetite?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Duermo mal. Me despierto varias veces en la noche. También como mucho menos y he perdido peso.

**English**

> I sleep poorly. I wake up several times at night. I also eat much less and I've lost weight.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> En una escala del cero al diez, donde diez es el peor ánimo imaginable, ¿cómo describiría su estado de ánimo la mayoría de los días?

**English**

> On a scale from zero to ten, where ten is the worst mood imaginable, how would you describe your mood on most days?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Es un ocho. Me cuesta concentrarme en el trabajo.

**English**

> It's an eight. I have trouble concentrating at work.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Estas molestias le están afectando en casa, en el trabajo o con su familia?

**English**

> Are these difficulties affecting you at home, at work, or with your family?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Sí. He faltado varios días al trabajo y casi no salgo con mis amigos.

**English**

> Yes. I've missed several days of work and I hardly go out with my friends anymore.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Para cuidar su seguridad, ¿ha tenido pensamientos de hacerse daño o de que sería mejor no estar vivo?

**English**

> To help keep you safe, have you had thoughts of hurting yourself or that it would be better not to be alive?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> No. No quiero hacerme daño. Solo me siento agotado y sin esperanza a veces.

**English**

> No. I don't want to hurt myself. I just feel exhausted and sometimes hopeless.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Gracias por compartirlo. ¿Ha tenido episodios similares en el pasado o está recibiendo algún tratamiento ahora?

**English**

> Thank you for sharing that. Have you had similar episodes in the past, or are you receiving any treatment now?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Hace años me sentí así después de perder un trabajo, pero nunca vi a un especialista. Ahora no tomo nada para el ánimo.

**English**

> Years ago I felt like this after losing a job, but I never saw a specialist. I'm not taking anything for mood now.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Está tomando algún otro medicamento actualmente?

**English**

> Are you currently taking any other medications?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Solo tomo omeprazol para la acidez.

**English**

> I only take omeprazole for heartburn.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> ¿Tiene alguna alergia a medicamentos?

**English**

> Do you have any medication allergies?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> No tengo alergias a medicamentos.

**English**

> I don't have any medication allergies.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Gracias. Permítame confirmar que entendí correctamente.
>
> Usted se siente triste casi todos los días desde hace unos dos meses, con poco interés en sus actividades habituales. Duerme mal, come menos y ha perdido peso. Califica su ánimo como ocho sobre diez y le cuesta concentrarse. Ha faltado al trabajo y ha reducido su vida social. No tiene pensamientos de hacerse daño. Tuvo un episodio similar en el pasado sin tratamiento especializado. Toma omeprazol y no tiene alergias a medicamentos.
>
> ¿Es correcto?

**English**

> Thank you. Let me confirm I understood correctly.
>
> You've felt sad almost every day for about two months, with little interest in your usual activities. You sleep poorly, eat less, and have lost weight. You rate your mood as eight out of ten and have trouble concentrating. You've missed work and reduced your social life. You do not have thoughts of harming yourself. You had a similar episode in the past without specialty treatment. You take omeprazole and have no medication allergies.
>
> Is that correct?

---

### Patient

**Spanish**

> Sí, es correcto.

**English**

> Yes, that's correct.

---

### Verbatim

**Spanish**

> Gracias. He preparado un resumen para su médico. Con base en los síntomas que ha informado, **su médico recomienda una evaluación con salud conductual / psiquiatría** para apoyar su estado de ánimo y funcionamiento. Nuestro personal le ayudará a programar la cita con el especialista.

**English**

> Thank you. I've prepared a summary for your physician. Based on the symptoms you've reported, **your physician recommends an evaluation with behavioral health / psychiatry** to support your mood and functioning. Our staff will help schedule your appointment with the specialist.

---

## Physician Summary

### Chief Complaint

- Persistent low mood and loss of interest

### Duration

- Approximately 2 months

### Severity

- Mood 8/10 (worst imaginable = 10)

### Symptoms

- Nearly daily sadness
- Anhedonia
- Poor sleep with nocturnal awakenings
- Decreased appetite with weight loss
- Impaired concentration
- Missed workdays; reduced social activity
- Occasional hopelessness
- Denies suicidal ideation or intent
- Prior similar episode years ago after job loss; no prior specialty care

### Medical History

- GERD / heartburn

### Medication

- Omeprazole

### Allergy

- None reported

---

## Referral

**Specialist:** Behavioral Health / Psychiatry

**Reason for Referral:**

- Persistent low mood and anhedonia for approximately two months
- Sleep and appetite disturbance with weight loss
- Functional impairment at work and socially
- Prior untreated depressive episode
- Negative screen for current suicidal ideation; specialty evaluation requested for mood and functioning

**Status:** Referral sent to Behavioral Health / Psychiatry for further evaluation.

---

## Clinical Safety Note

Verbatim supports intake, translation, summarization, and clinical decision support. Final diagnosis, treatment, and referral decisions must be reviewed and approved by a licensed healthcare professional. Safety screening during intake does not replace clinical judgment; any positive safety concerns require immediate clinician review.
