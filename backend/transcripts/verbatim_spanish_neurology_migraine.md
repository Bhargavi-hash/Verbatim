## Physician Summary

### Chief Complaint

- Recurrent severe headaches

### Duration

- Approximately 6 months

### Severity

- 9/10

### Symptoms

- Frequency: 4–5 times per month
- Visual aura (zigzag lights ~20 minutes before pain)
- Nausea
- Photophobia and phonophobia
- Needs dark room during attacks
- No weakness, speech difficulty, confusion, or balance loss
- Limited relief with ibuprofen

### Medical History

- On oral contraceptive

### Medication

- Ibuprofen as needed
- Oral contraceptive

### Allergy

- None reported

## Referral

**Specialist:** Neurology

**Reason for Referral:**

- Recurrent severe headaches for approximately six months
- Frequency of four to five episodes per month
- Visual aura preceding pain
- Associated nausea, photophobia, and phonophobia
- Inadequate relief with over-the-counter ibuprofen

**Status:** Referral sent to Neurology for further evaluation.
